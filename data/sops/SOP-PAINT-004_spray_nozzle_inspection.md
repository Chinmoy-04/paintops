# SOP-PAINT-004 — Spray Nozzle Inspection (Synthetic)

**Document ID:** SOP-PAINT-004  
**Title:** Spray Nozzle Visual Inspection  

> **SYNTHETIC TRAINING EXAMPLE — NOT AN APPROVED INDUSTRIAL SOP**  
> Fictional procedure for portfolio demonstration only.

## Purpose

Outline a synthetic maintenance inspection checklist for spray nozzles when coverage or texture defects recur.

## Applicable station

Spray robots serving `PAINT-LINE-01` and `PAINT-LINE-02` (synthetic).

## Example defect symptoms

- Uneven coverage / thin spots
- Runs and sags on vertical panels
- Orange peel when atomisation quality declines

## Inspection checks

1. Visual check for nozzle fouling or tip wear (demo checklist).
2. Confirm recent defect pattern is booth-local vs line-wide.
3. Capture hypothesis language carefully — never claim confirmed root cause without human review.

## Possible contributing factors (hypotheses only)

- Partial nozzle blockage
- Incorrect spray distance (synthetic)
- Atomising air pressure drift

## Suggested escalation / review steps

Draft a `maintenance_inspection` ticket when recurrence or high severity warrants review. Pause for human approval.
