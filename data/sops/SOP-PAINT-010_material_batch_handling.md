# SOP-PAINT-010 — Material Batch Handling (Synthetic)

**Document ID:** SOP-PAINT-010  
**Title:** Material Batch Change Review  

> **SYNTHETIC TRAINING EXAMPLE — NOT AN APPROVED INDUSTRIAL SOP**  
> Fictional procedure for portfolio demonstration only.

## Purpose

Describe a synthetic review of paint material batches when defects begin after a batch change.

## Applicable station

All synthetic paint booths on `PAINT-LINE-01` … `PAINT-LINE-03`.

## Example defect symptoms

- Defects starting within a few hours of a new batch being loaded
- Same defect type on several booths that received the same batch
- Batch viscosity recorded outside the demo band of 22–26 s

## Inspection checks

1. Identify the active batch for the booth at the time of the event.
2. Compare batch start time with the time of the first related defect.
3. Check the recorded viscosity against the demo band.
4. Check whether other booths received the same batch.

## Possible contributing factors (hypotheses only)

- Batch viscosity outside the demo band (synthetic)
- Supplier or formulation change
- Contamination during batch changeover

## Suggested escalation / review steps

Draft a `quality_inspection` ticket recommending a batch review and possible quality hold (SOP-PAINT-006). Pause for human approval.
