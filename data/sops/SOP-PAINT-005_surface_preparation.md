## Purpose

Provide synthetic guidance when uneven coverage or adhesion-related appearance issues are reported.

## Applicable station

Pre-paint prep and flash-off zones feeding the paint booths (fictional).

## Example defect symptoms

- Uneven coverage
- Patchy film build
- Isolated defects without strong environmental correlation

## Inspection checks

1. Review whether the defect is isolated or recurring.
2. Note body ID sequence for possible prep-station correlation (demo only).
3. Cross-check SOP-PAINT-001 classification.

## Possible contributing factors (hypotheses only)

- Incomplete wipe-down
- Contaminated substrate
- Flash time variation (synthetic)

## Suggested escalation / review steps

Prefer monitor-only for single low/medium events. Escalate when recurrence threshold is met.
