# SOP-PAINT-003 — Booth Environment Checks (Synthetic)

**Document ID:** SOP-PAINT-003  
**Title:** Paint Booth Environment Checks  

> **SYNTHETIC TRAINING EXAMPLE — NOT AN APPROVED INDUSTRIAL SOP**  
> Demo-only content. Not validated for real booth control.

## Purpose

Describe fictional environmental checks for temperature, humidity, and airflow when paint defects recur.

## Applicable station

All synthetic paint booths (`BOOTH-01` … `BOOTH-08`).

## Example defect symptoms

- Multiple defect types appearing during humidity spikes
- Texture defects coinciding with low airflow readings
- Temperature drift noted in inspection telemetry

## Inspection checks

1. Compare telemetry against the synthetic demo bands used in fixtures.
2. Note whether multiple bodies in the same booth share similar environmental readings.
3. Record findings in the investigation evidence list — do not adjust setpoints automatically.

## Possible contributing factors (hypotheses only)

- HVAC imbalance
- Door/curtain leakage introducing humid air
- Sensor calibration drift (synthetic)

## Suggested escalation / review steps

Include booth environment evidence in any action ticket draft. Require human approval before maintenance inspection.
