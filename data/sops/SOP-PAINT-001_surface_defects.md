**Document ID:** SOP-PAINT-001  
**Title:** Surface Defect Classification Overview  

> **SYNTHETIC TRAINING EXAMPLE — NOT AN APPROVED INDUSTRIAL SOP**  
> This document is a fictional training artefact for the PaintOps portfolio demo. It must not be used for real production quality decisions.

## Purpose

Provide a synthetic overview of common paint-surface defect categories observed at a fictional automotive paint-inspection station.

## Applicable station

Synthetic CV inspection station on `PAINT-LINE-01` / `PAINT-LINE-02`.

## Example defect symptoms

- Orange peel texture or micro-waviness on cured film
- Runs / sags on vertical panels
- Dust or particulate inclusions trapped in the clearcoat
- Uneven coverage / thin spots

## Inspection checks

1. Confirm defect category assigned by the inspection system.
2. Note booth ID, body ID, and environmental telemetry.
3. Compare against recent same-booth history for recurrence.

## Possible contributing factors (hypotheses only)

- Booth humidity or airflow outside the synthetic demo band
- Nozzle wear or spray pattern drift
- Incomplete surface preparation
- Contaminated booth air

## Suggested escalation / review steps

Escalate to human quality review when the same defect recurs in one booth within a short window, or when severity is marked high/critical in the synthetic analysis.
